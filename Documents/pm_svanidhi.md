# PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)

## Overview
PM SVANidhi is a special micro-credit facility launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to provide affordable collateral-free working capital loans to street vendors affected by economic disruption.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Beneficiaries:** Urban street vendors, hawkers, thelawalas, rehri-patri vendors selling goods like vegetables, fruits, ready-to-eat street food, tea, apparel, artisan products, barbers, and cobblers.
- **Location:** Vendors vending in urban areas, peri-urban, and rural surrounding areas.
- **Documents:** Certificate of Vending (CoV) / Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) or Letter of Recommendation (LoR).
- **Age:** 18 years and above.

## Loan Tranches and Benefits
- **1st Tranche Loan:** Up to ₹10,000 with a 1-year repayment tenure.
- **2nd Tranche Loan:** Up to ₹20,000 on timely or early repayment of the 1st loan (tenure 18 months).
- **3rd Tranche Loan:** Up to ₹50,000 on timely repayment of the 2nd loan (tenure 36 months).
- **Interest Subsidy:** 7% interest subsidy credited directly to the vendor's bank account quarterly upon regular repayments.
- **Digital Cash Back:** Monthly cashback up to ₹100 (up to ₹1,200 annually) for conducting digital UPI transactions.
- **Collateral:** Zero collateral or guarantor required.

## How to Apply
1. Check eligibility on `https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in`.
2. Apply through Common Service Centers (CSC), Urban Local Bodies, or banking correspondents.
3. Link mobile with Aadhaar for Aadhaar-based e-KYC.
