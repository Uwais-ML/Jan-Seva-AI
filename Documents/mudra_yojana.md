# Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) provides collateral-free loans up to ₹10 Lakh (recently expanded to ₹20 Lakh under Union Budget for past borrowers) to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises.

## Categories of MUDRA Loans
1. **Shishu:** Loans up to ₹50,000 (ideal for new startups, small shops, roadside stalls).
2. **Kishore:** Loans above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5,00,000 (for purchasing equipment, machinery, inventory).
3. **Tarun:** Loans above ₹5,00,000 and up to ₹10,00,000 / ₹20,00,000 (for expansion and scaling up business).

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Age:** 18 years to 65 years.
- **Beneficiaries:** Any Indian citizen with a viable business plan for manufacturing, processing, trading, or service sector activities.
- **Excluded:** Large corporations and agricultural farming (allied activities like dairy, poultry, fishery are included).
- **Collateral:** No collateral security or third-party guarantee required.

## Benefits
- No processing fee for Shishu loans.
- Access to formal banking credit without pledging physical assets.
- MUDRA Card (RuPay Debit Card) provided for working capital withdrawals.
