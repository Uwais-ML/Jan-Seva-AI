# Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 4.0)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) is the flagship scheme of the Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (MSDE) implemented by National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC). The objective is to enable Indian youth to take up industry-relevant skill training that will help them secure a better livelihood.

## Key Objectives
- Provide short-term skill training, recognition of prior learning (RPL), and special projects.
- Align training with National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF).
- Foster entrepreneurship and job placement for certified trainees.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Age:** 15 to 45 years.
- **Nationality:** Citizen of India with valid Aadhaar card.
- **Education:** Open to candidates with basic school education (8th/10th/12th pass) or college dropouts looking for vocational skills.
- **Employment Status:** Unemployed youth or school/college dropouts.
- **Income Limit:** No strict upper income limit, but preference is given to economically disadvantaged youth.
- **Applicable States:** All States and Union Territories across India.

## Benefits and Financial Assistance
- 100% free skill training sponsored by the Government.
- Government certification recognized across India and globally.
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) stipend for eligible candidates during training.
- Placement assistance and post-placement support allowance.
- Accidental insurance coverage for 3 years for certified candidates.

## Sectors and Courses Covered
- Information Technology (IT-ITeS)
- Healthcare and Nursing Assistance
- Automotive and Electric Vehicles (EV)
- Agriculture and Food Processing
- Electronics and Hardware
- Apparel, Textiles, and Handloom
- Solar and Renewable Energy Installation

## How to Apply
1. Visit the official Skill India Digital portal at `https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in`.
2. Register using mobile number and Aadhaar authentication.
3. Select desired skill course and nearest PMKVY Training Centre.
4. Complete the training and pass the assessment to receive certification and placement support.
