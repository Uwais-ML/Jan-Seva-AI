# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

## Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a Government of India backed small savings scheme initiated under the "Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao" campaign to build a fund for the education and marriage of a girl child.

## Key Rules & Eligibility Criteria
- **Age of Girl Child:** Up to 10 years at the time of opening the account (with 1 year grace period during initial launch).
- **Nationality:** Indian resident girl child.
- **Account Limit:** Maximum of two accounts per family (one for each girl child). Exception for triplets or twins born in first or second delivery.
- **Guardian:** Account opened and operated by natural or legal guardian until girl reaches 18 years.

## Financial Terms and Benefits
- **Minimum Deposit:** ₹250 per financial year.
- **Maximum Deposit:** ₹1,50,000 per financial year.
- **Interest Rate:** High sovereign interest rate (currently ~8.2% per annum, compounded annually).
- **Tax Benefit:** Triple E (EEE) tax exemption under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act (deposit exempt, interest exempt, maturity amount exempt).
- **Tenure:** Account matures after 21 years from date of opening or upon marriage of the girl child after age 18.
- **Partial Withdrawal:** Up to 50% of the balance permitted for higher education after girl reaches 18 years or passes 10th standard.

## How to Open Account
- Open at any Post Office or authorized Commercial Bank (SBI, PNB, BoB, etc.) with girl's birth certificate and guardian's KYC.

## Official Portal & Source
- Portal: India Post / Reserve Bank of India
- Ministry: Ministry of Finance / Ministry of Women and Child Development
