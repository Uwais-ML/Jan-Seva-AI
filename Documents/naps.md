# National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS-2)

## Overview
National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) aims to promote apprenticeship training in the country, providing financial support to enterprises and stipends to youth undergoing industrial on-the-job training.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Age:** Minimum 14 years (18 years for designated hazardous industries). Upper age usually not strictly capped, typically youth aged 14 to 35.
- **Educational Qualification:** 5th pass, 8th pass, 10th pass, 12th pass, ITI pass, Diploma, or Degree holders depending on the designated trade.
- **Aadhaar:** Aadhaar linked bank account is mandatory for receiving Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) stipend.

## Key Benefits
- **Hands-on Industry Exposure:** Practical experience in manufacturing, automotive, electronics, logistics, retail, healthcare, etc.
- **Government Stipend Support:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of 25% of prescribed stipend up to ₹1,500 per month per apprentice directly to the apprentice's bank account.
- **Recognized Certification:** National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) issued by NCVT / MSDE upon completion.

## How to Apply
1. Register on the Apprenticeship India Portal `https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in`.
2. Complete e-KYC and profile creation.
3. Search and apply for apprenticeship vacancies across registered companies and public sector undertakings.

## Official Portal & Source
- Official Portal: https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in
- Ministry: Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE)
