# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

## Overview
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded health assurance scheme. It provides health cover of ₹5,00,000 per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across public and empaneled private hospitals in India.

## Key Objectives
- Protect vulnerable and economically poor families from catastrophic health expenditures.
- Provide cashless and paperless access to healthcare services at empaneled hospitals.
- Strengthen public healthcare infrastructure and primary health centers (Ayushman Arogya Mandirs).

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Age:** No restriction on age (all family members covered).
- **Family Size:** No cap on family size or gender.
- **Economic Criteria:** Based on Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) criteria:
  - Rural families living in kutcha houses, households without adult male members (16-59 years), female-headed households with no adult male member, SC/ST households, landless casual manual workers.
  - Urban categories: Ragpickers, domestic workers, street vendors, cobblers, construction workers, plumbers, transport workers, delivery workers.
  - State ration card holders under Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) and Priority Household (PHH).
- **Income Limit:** Generally annual family income below ₹2,50,000 or meeting BPL/SECC deprivation indicators.
- **Senior Citizens 70+:** Recently expanded to cover all senior citizens aged 70 and above, regardless of income, under a distinct Ayushman Vaya Vandana card with ₹5 Lakh health cover.
- **Applicable States:** All States and UTs except where state-specific schemes operate independently (e.g. West Bengal, Odisha - though some now integrating).

## Benefits Provided
- Cashless hospitalization cover of up to ₹5 Lakh per year per family.
- Covers medical examination, consultation, pre-hospitalization tests (up to 3 days), post-hospitalization medicine and care (up to 15 days).
- Covers more than 1,949 medical and surgical procedures including oncology, cardiology, neurosurgery, and orthopedics.
- All pre-existing health conditions are covered from day one.

## How to Check Eligibility and Apply
1. Visit the official portal `https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in` or download the Ayushman App.
2. Enter mobile number, Aadhaar number, or Ration Card number to search family records.
3. If eligible, complete e-KYC using Aadhaar OTP or biometric authentication.
4. Download the digital Ayushman Card immediately.

## Official Portal & Source
- Official Portal: https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in
- Authority: National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)
