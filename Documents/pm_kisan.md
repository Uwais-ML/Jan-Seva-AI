# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

## Overview
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme launched to supplement the financial needs of landholding farmers in procuring inputs for agricultural and allied activities, as well as domestic needs.

## Key Objectives
- Augment farmers' income and prevent distress selling.
- Support timely input purchases for Kharif and Rabi cropping seasons.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Beneficiaries:** All landholding farmer families having cultivable land in their name.
- **Exclusion Criteria:**
  - Institutional landholders.
  - Farmer families holding constitutional posts.
  - Present or retired officers/employees of State/Central Government and PSUs (excluding multi-tasking staff / Class IV).
  - Income tax payers of last assessment year.
  - Doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, and architects registered with professional bodies.
  - Monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more for retired pensioners.
- **Applicable States:** All States and UTs across India.

## Benefits Provided
- Financial benefit of ₹6,000 per year transferred directly to bank accounts.
- Paid in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every four months:
  - Period 1: April to July
  - Period 2: August to November
  - Period 3: December to March
- 100% funding by the Government of India via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## How to Apply
1. Visit the PM-KISAN portal at `https://pmkisan.gov.in`.
2. Click on 'New Farmer Registration'.
3. Enter Aadhaar number, mobile number, and select rural or urban farmer.
4. Upload land records (Khasra/Khatauni) and bank account details.
5. Mandatory e-KYC must be completed using Aadhaar OTP or facial recognition via PM-KISAN mobile app.

## Official Portal & Source
- Official Portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in
- Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
